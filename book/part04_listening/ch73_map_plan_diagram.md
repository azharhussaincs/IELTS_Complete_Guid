## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

- Orient yourself quickly on maps, floor plans, site plans, and process diagrams before audio begins.
- Follow directional language (left, right, opposite, beyond, adjacent) under time pressure.
- Match labelled options from a box to diagram locations without wasting time rewriting words.
- Track multi-step routes and sequential process stages in audio order.
- Distinguish map labelling (Sections 1–2) from academic diagram tasks (Sections 2–4).
- Avoid classic traps: mirror confusion, starting-point shifts, and recycled option letters.
- Label unfamiliar spaces using landmark anchoring and compass-style mental models.

## Theory / Explanation

### 73.1 Task Types in This Family

IELTS Listening uses visual-spatial tasks in several forms:

| Format | Visual | Typical section | Answer style |
|--------|--------|-----------------|--------------|
| Map labelling | Town/campus/park map | 1–2 | Word from list OR short phrase |
| Plan labelling | Building floor plan | 2 | Letter or word |
| Diagram labelling | Process/equipment diagram | 2–4 | Word from box |
| Flow chart stages | Sequential boxes | 4 | Gap fill |

All require **linking spoken description to spatial or sequential position**.

### 73.2 Why Maps Appear in Section 2

Section 2 often features a **monologue tour** — museum guide, campus orientation, festival layout. Speaker walks listener through space. Questions test:

- **Directional comprehension**
- **Landmark reference** (*past the fountain*, *behind the café*)
- **Renaming / temporary changes** (*what used to be the gift shop is now…*)

### 73.3 Pre-Listening Orientation Protocol (60 Seconds)

**Step 1 — Locate YOU ARE HERE** or implicit entrance (often bottom-centre).

**Step 2 — Identify **north** if shown; if not, assign **mental north** upward on page.

**Step 3 — Mark **fixed landmarks** already labelled (car park, river, main gate).

**Step 4 — Read **option list** (A–H) — note word class (building types, facilities).

**Step 5 — Predict **route start** from first question instruction.

**Why 60 seconds matters:** Audio begins walking immediately; unprepared candidates chase the speaker visually.

### 73.4 Directional Language Lexicon

| Expression | Meaning |
|------------|---------|
| On your left/right | Relative to speaker's facing direction |
| Opposite / facing | Across a path or road |
| Adjacent / next to | Sharing a boundary |
| At the corner of | Intersection of two paths/streets |
| Beyond / past | Farther along same route |
| In the middle of | Central position |
| At the far end | Maximum distance on axis |
| Diagonally opposite | Across and offset |
| Fork / branch | Path splits |
| Roundabout / circle | Circular junction |

**Critical:** Speaker's left may differ from your map orientation if they face toward you. **Follow movement direction** on the map step by step.

### 73.5 Letter-from-Box vs Write-the-Word

Two answer modes:

**Mode A — Choose letter:**
Map shows gaps 11–14; list A–H at bottom. Write **letter only** on answer sheet unless instructed otherwise.

**Mode B — Write word:**
No letters; write exact word from list (spelling must match list).

**Exam tip:** Never write both letter and word unless required — wastes time and risks ambiguity.

### 73.6 Process and Equipment Diagrams

Section 4 may describe **scientific apparatus** or **natural cycles**:

- Water treatment stages
- Life cycle of insect
- Parts of hearing mechanism

**Strategy:**
1. Identify **entry point** (input/raw material).
2. Follow ** arrows** if present.
3. Note **parallel paths** (split flows confuse).

Audio describes stages **in sequence** — usually matches question order.

### 73.7 Distractors in Spatial Tasks

1. **Mentioned but not answer:** Speaker passes café en route to answer (library).
2. **Temporary relocation:** "The clinic has moved to…"
3. **Double naming:** "Old gymnasium, now called Sports Hall B"
4. **Option reuse prohibition:** Each letter used once in many tasks — elimination helps.
5. **Dead-end paths:** Speaker describes wrong turn then corrects.

### 73.8 Note-Taking on Maps

Light pencil marks during audio:
- Arrow path along route
- Cross out eliminated options
- Circle final labels

Erase clutter before transfer if PBT.

### 73.9 Integration with Vocabulary

Map tasks favour **facility nouns:**

| Domain | Lexis |
|--------|-------|
| Campus | lecture theatre, refectory, accommodation block |
| Town | pedestrian zone, bypass, roundabout |
| Nature | wetland, boardwalk, viewing platform |
| Museum | gallery, atrium, temporary exhibition space |

Study Chapter 63 (Transportation) and Chapter 57 (Tourism) for collocations.

### 73.10 Accessibility and Scale

Some maps lack scale bars. Use **relative distance language:**

- *"Just a short walk"* ≈ adjacent
- *"At the far side of the field"* ≈ opposite edge

**Why:** Absolute metres rarely appear; relational phrases dominate.

### 73.11 Common Errors and Fixes

| Error | Fix |
|-------|-----|
| Mirror left/right | Trace speaker path with finger |
| Lost start point | Reset at labelled landmark each question group |
| Wrong option reused | Track used letters in margin |
| Missed "used to be" | Circle tense shift verbs in preview |

### 73.12 Practice Progression

1. **Static maps:** label from description without walking audio (teacher reads).
2. **Guided tours:** podcast city walks with map PDFs.
3. **Timed IELTS sections:** strict one-play.
4. **Blind draw:** listen, sketch route, compare — builds mental mapping.

## Examples

### Example 1: Relative Position

**Audio:** *"The gift shop is opposite the main entrance, on the other side of the courtyard."*

If entrance at south, shop at north across courtyard — not adjacent east/west.

### Example 2: Process Diagram

Stages: Collection → Screening → Sedimentation → Filtration → Disinfection

**Audio skips explicit "screening" but mentions *"removing large debris"* — maps to **screening** box.

### Example 3: Correction

**Audio:** *"Turn left at the rose garden — sorry, I mean right — toward the lake."*

Answer path uses **right turn**.

## Worked Examples

### Worked Example 1: Campus Map (Letters A–F)

**Options:** A Library, B Gym, C Pharmacy, D Bus stop, E Pond, F Accommodation

**Audio excerpt:** *"Starting from the bus stop, walk straight past the library on your right. The gym is the large building facing the pond, not the one near accommodation."*

**Question 11:** Building facing pond → **B (Gym)**

**WHY not F:** "Near accommodation" describes a different building (distractor).

### Worked Example 2: Festival Plan

Gaps: First aid, Food court, Stage 2

**Audio:** *"Beyond the main stage, follow the path that curves left. Food stalls cluster at the bend. First aid is back near the entrance, beside the information kiosk."*

Mapping:
- Food court → at bend (left curve)
- First aid → entrance/kiosk
- Stage 2 → inferred main stage already labelled; Stage 2 opposite field per earlier context

### Worked Example 3: Diagram — Bee Anatomy (Section 4 style)

**Audio:** *"Pollen collects on the **hind legs** in structures called corbiculae, often visible as yellow pellets."*

Gap label on leg diagram → **hind legs** (must match word box if provided).

## Practice Exercises

### Practice 1 — Riverside Park Map (Section 2)

**Speaker tour transcript (original):**

> Welcome to Riverside Park. Enter through the **south gate**. Immediately on your left is the **visitor centre**. Walk north along the main path; the **playground** is on the east side, beside the café. Continue to the **footbridge** crossing the river. On the far northern bank, you'll find the **bird hide**. The **car park** remains outside the south gate, across Mill Road. Cyclists should note the **bike rack** sits between the visitor centre and the playground.

**Map gaps (label from list):**
- South gate area (off-map): Car park = ___
- Left of path on entry: ___
- East side mid-park: ___
- Crosses river: ___
- North bank: ___
- Between visitor centre and playground: ___

**Word box:** visitor centre, playground, footbridge, bird hide, car park, bike rack

### Practice 2 — Museum Floor Plan

**Transcript (original):**

> Level one: Temporary exhibitions occupy the **atrium** — that's the open space just inside the entrance. Permanent sculpture is **west wing**, upstairs via the grand staircase. The **café** moved last month to the east wing ground floor, replacing the bookshop. Restrooms remain **adjacent to the cloakroom**, north of the atrium.

**Questions:**
1. Open space at entrance: ___________
2. Sculpture location: ___________
3. Café now in: ___________
4. Restrooms location: ___________

### Practice 3 — Water Treatment Diagram (Section 4)

**Transcript (original):**

> Raw water enters the **intake** screen. Fine particles settle in **tanks** after coagulation chemicals are added. Clear water passes through **sand filters** before **chlorine** is injected. Finally, water enters the **reservoir** for distribution.

**Stages (in order):** intake → ___ → settling tanks → ___ → ___ → reservoir

**Word box:** sand filters, chlorine, pumping station (distractor), coagulation (may be implied)

### Practice 4 — University Orientation (Multiple choice map)

**Transcript (original):**

> The science faculty building sits **diagonally opposite** the student union, across the plaza. The medical library is **behind** the hospital block, not beside the pharmacy school as old maps show. Buses stop on **West Lane**, not East Lane since the roadworks.

### Practice 5 — Diagram Labelling Practice

Label parts of a **volcano cross-section** from lecture:

> Magma rises through the central **vent**. Gas and ash erupt from the **crater** at the summit. Secondary openings called **side vents** may form on flanks. Molten rock stored underground lies in the **magma chamber** deep below.

## Review

- **Orient first** — entrance, north, landmarks.
- Trace route **with finger** during audio.
- Directional language is precise; **corrections** happen.
- Diagrams follow **process order**.
- Option boxes: **each letter once** (usually).
- Facility vocabulary from topic chapters supports prediction.

## Exam Tips

> **Exam Strategy:** If lost, **wait for the next landmark** — speakers re-anchor ("now we're back at the fountain").

> **Exam Strategy:** For box lists, **write the letter** on the answer sheet if instructions specify letters only.

> **Exam Strategy:** Diagram Section 4: **skim all gap labels** before audio — know if gaps are parts, stages, or chemicals.

## Band 9 Tips

> **Band 9 Secret:** Band 9 mappers **assign temporary nicknames** in pencil (*VC*, *PH*) to unlabelled gaps before audio — faster than re-reading full words.

> **Band 9 Secret:** They **eliminate box options aggressively** when a facility is mentioned but already placed — narrows remaining choices logically.

> **Band 9 Secret:** For process diagrams, they **number arrows** 1–6 in preview so audio triggers slot into pre-marked slots without re-scanning the whole image.
