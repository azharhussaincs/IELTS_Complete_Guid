## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

- Identify the four MCQ formats used in IELTS Reading and adapt strategy to each.
- Apply systematic distractor elimination using the TRUE/FALSE/NG logic framework.
- Distinguish main-idea questions from detail questions and writer-attitude questions.
- Avoid extreme options, partial-quote options, and true-but-irrelevant options.
- Locate answer zones efficiently when questions are ordered or scattered.
- Handle multi-answer MCQ items ("Choose TWO letters") without double-counting errors.
- Complete MCQ sets at approximately 1.5 minutes per question under exam conditions.

## Theory / Explanation

### 86.1 MCQ Formats in IELTS Reading

**Format A — Single answer (A/B/C/D or A/B/C/D/E)**  
Most common. One correct option.

**Format B — Choose TWO (or THREE)**  
"Which TWO advantages are mentioned?" — five or six options listed; select 2. Each correct selection = 1 mark.

**Format C — Question first, options after passage section**  
Grouped with related paragraphs.

**Format D — Complete the question stem**  
" The writer suggests that the primary cause of X is..." + 4 completions.

**Why format awareness matters:** Multi-select items require **confirmation of each option** against text — not stop after finding one correct.

### 86.2 Question Type Subcategories

| Subcategory | What it asks | Strategy |
|-------------|--------------|----------|
| Detail | Specific fact/intention | Scan + intensive read |
| Main idea | Paragraph/passage gist | Skim + reject detail options |
| Writer's purpose/reason | Why author includes information | Read surrounding discourse |
| Inference | Implied but not stated | Caution — closest supported inference only |
| Attitude/tone | Writer's view | YNNG-style evaluative language |

### 86.3 The Elimination Framework

Treat each option as a **mini TFNG statement:**

| Option status | Action |
|---------------|--------|
| Contradicted by passage | Eliminate |
| Not mentioned | Eliminate |
| Partially true but incomplete | Usually eliminate (IELTS prefers complete match) |
| Fully supported paraphrase | Strong candidate |
| True in real world but not in passage | Eliminate |

**Two finalists:** Choose the option that matches **question stem focus** — not the more interesting option.

### 86.4 The Six Distractor Types

**1. Word-copy distractor** — Repeats passage vocabulary but reverses meaning.

**2. True elsewhere** — Correct for different paragraph, not question scope.

**3. Absolute distractor** — all, never, completely — when passage hedges.

**4. Detail distractor** — True fact, wrong level (example not main idea).

**5. Opposite distractor** — Antonym or reversed causality.

**6. Attractive inference** — Logical but unsupported — **NG option**.

### 86.5 Reading the Stem First

**Always read question stem before options** — stems contain:

- Scope limiters ("according to the passage," "in paragraph 3")
- Target ("main reason," "primary concern," "writer's attitude")

**Without stem focus:** detail option wins when main-idea required.

### 86.6 Multi-Select Protocol (Choose TWO)

1. Read all options quickly — mark clearly wrong.
2. Scan passage for each remaining option — confirm mentioned.
3. Select exactly required number — **order on answer sheet irrelevant**.
4. If only one confirmed, re-scan for second — don't guess contradictory pair.

**Common error:** Pick two options that contradict each other when passage supports only one.

### 86.7 Location and Order

Single-answer MCQ usually **follows passage order**. Use previous question location as lower bound for scan.

**Exception:** Main idea question about whole passage — may require global skim.

### 86.8 Time and Marking

~**1.5 minutes per MCQ** — longer than TFNG due to four-option evaluation.

**Do not over-invest:** If torn between two after 2 minutes, mark best guess, flag, move.

### 86.9 Comparison with Listening MCQ

Reading MCQ allows **re-scanning text**; Listening does not. Reading candidates should **verify every option** in text when time permits — not rely on memory.

## Examples

### Example 1: Detail MCQ

**Question:** Why did the 2018 trial fail?  
**Passage:** "...trial aborted due to insufficient sample diversity."

**A.** Equipment malfunction  
**B.** Lack of varied participants ✓  
**C.** Regulatory ban  
**D.** Funding withdrawal  

Eliminate A, C, D — not supported. B paraphrases insufficient sample diversity.

### Example 2: Main Idea Elimination

**Question:** What is the main point of paragraph 4?

**A.** A 1992 conference in Geneva (detail)  
**B.** The shift from voluntary to mandatory reporting (gist) ✓  
**C.** Statistics on carbon emissions (evidence)  
**D.** Criticism from industry lobbyists (counterpoint detail)

### Example 3: Choose TWO

**Which TWO benefits of urban trees are stated?**

Passage mentions: air quality improvement, shade reducing energy costs, habitat for birds, aesthetic pleasure for residents.

Options list six items including "reduced crime rates" (not stated) and "shade reducing energy costs" (stated).

Confirm each option individually against text.

## Worked Examples

### Worked Example 1: Writer's Attitude

**Passage:** "The proposal is, frankly, an impractical distraction from simpler reforms."

**Question:** The writer's view of the proposal?

**A.** Neutral description  
**B.** Enthusiastic support  
**C.** Strong disapproval ✓  
**D.** Uncertain optimism  

"Frankly" + "impractical distraction" = strong disapproval.

### Worked Example 2: Inference Boundaries

**Passage:** "Without intervention, the language may lose its last fluent speakers within a generation."

**Question:** What can be inferred?

**A.** The language is extinct now — FALSE (future may)  
**B.** Young people currently reject the language — NG  
**C.** Urgent action could plausibly affect survival ✓ — reasonable inference from "without intervention" implying intervention might help  
**D.** Government has already funded programmes — NG  

### Worked Example 3: Multi-Select

**Which TWO challenges does the author mention for deep-sea mining?**

Passage: ecological disturbance; high operational costs; legal uncertainty about seabed ownership. Also notes technological progress in drilling.

Options include ecological disturbance, costs, ownership law, worker training (not mentioned), tourism impact (not mentioned).

**Answers:** ecological disturbance + costs (or ownership — verify text lists all three; pick two explicitly emphasised).

## Exercises / Passages

### Passage: Vertical Farming

Vertical farms stack crop layers indoors under LED spectra tuned to plant photoreceptors. Proponents argue that controlled environments eliminate seasonal volatility and reduce transport emissions because production can sit adjacent to consumers. Critics counter that energy demands for lighting often exceed savings from reduced trucking, particularly where grids rely on coal. A 2020 meta-analysis by Okonkwo and Lee found net energy outcomes highly sensitive to local electricity sources, cautioning against universal claims. Nevertheless, Singapore and several Gulf cities have subsidised pilot towers to bolster food security in import-dependent economies.

**Questions 1–7**

1. Vertical farming primarily aims to maximise crop genetic modification.  
   A. True according to passage  
   B. False according to passage  
   C. Not discussed — choose best MCQ format answer from:  
   A. Genetic modification focus  
   B. Controlled indoor layered cultivation ✓ (implicit main design)  
   C. Elimination of all pesticides  
   D. Replacement of all rural agriculture  

2. According to the passage, critics believe vertical farms may be  
   A. too dependent on favourable weather  
   B. energy-intensive relative to benefits ✓  
   C. unable to grow leafy vegetables  
   D. legally prohibited in coal regions  

3. Okonkwo and Lee's study suggests that  
   A. vertical farming always saves energy  
   B. outcomes depend on power source type ✓  
   C. trucking emissions are irrelevant  
   D. Singapore rejected pilot projects  

4. The writer mentions Singapore in order to  
   A. illustrate government support despite debates ✓  
   B. prove coal grids favour vertical farms  
   C. criticise import-dependent economies  
   D. describe LED technology  

5. Which TWO factors do proponents emphasise? (Choose TWO)  
   A. Seasonal stability ✓  
   B. Lower transport emissions ✓  
   C. Coal-powered grids  
   D. Guaranteed profit  
   E. Elimination of critics  

6–7. Short MCQ concept checks:  
6. Absolute option "always" when passage says "highly sensitive" → distractor type?  
7. Option copying "LED spectra" without answering "why Singapore" → distractor type?

---

## Solutions

**1.** **B** — Controlled indoor layered cultivation; genetic modification not mentioned (A wrong); C, D absolutes unsupported.

**2.** **B** — Critics: energy demands often exceed savings.

**3.** **B** — Net energy outcomes sensitive to local electricity sources.

**4.** **A** — Subsidised pilots despite debates (Okonkwo caution + nevertheless subsidised).

**5.** **A and B** — Proponents: eliminate seasonal volatility + reduce transport emissions.

**6.** Type 3 — absolute distractor  
**7.** Type 1 — word-copy / irrelevant detail

## Review

- Read **stem first** — scope and question type.
- Eliminate using **TFNG logic** on each option.
- Six distractor types: word-copy, true elsewhere, absolute, detail, opposite, over-inference.
- **Choose TWO:** verify each option independently.
- ~1.5 min each; flag and move if stuck.
- Main idea ≠ detail option.

## Exam Tips

1. **Cross out eliminated options** on question paper.
2. **Underline stem qualifiers** — "main," "primary," "according to passage."
3. **Never select from outside knowledge.**
4. **Choose TWO:** write both letters; check both marked.
5. **If two seem correct** for single-answer — re-read stem for scope.
6. **Extreme language** — almost always wrong unless passage extreme.
7. **Verify winner** — selected option should have textual evidence.
8. **Paragraph-number stem** — don't read whole passage.
9. **Return to flagged** MCQ in Pass 3 with fresh elimination.
10. **Listening MCQ skills** (Ch 74) transfer — same distractor logic.

## Band 9 Strategies

1. **Predict answer before viewing options** — Band 9 default habit.

2. **Option parallel structure scan** — grammatical asymmetry sometimes eliminates one option instantly.

3. **Negation in options:** `not`, `without` — double-check against passage; common trap.

4. **Scope pin:** tie answer to exact paragraph if stem specifies — zero leakage from other sections.

5. **Multi-select completeness:** confirm **exactly** required number; leaving one blank loses mark.

6. **Inference ceiling:** choose **minimum inference** supported — not deepest interpretation.

7. **Discourse function questions:** ask why author mentions X — answer often "to illustrate Y" found in adjacent sentences.

8. **Time cap 90 seconds** for standard MCQ — Band 9 candidates move decisively.

9. **Review only flagged MCQ** — no re-read all options pass.

10. **Log distractor type** — pattern recognition accelerates future elimination.

### 86.10 Writer Purpose Questions

**Format:** "The writer mentions X in order to..."

**Answer types:**

- Illustrate a broader claim  
- Provide counterevidence  
- Define a key term  
- Contrast with previous view  

**Method:** Read two sentences **before and after** mention X — writer's logical move usually visible in transition words.

### 86.11 Except/Not Questions (Rare)

**Format:** "All of the following are mentioned EXCEPT..."

**Strategy:** Verify four options against text — three present, one absent. Three true elsewhere distractors common — eliminate confirmed mentions first.

### 86.12 Long Stem MCQ

Some stems exceed two lines — **underline core question** (final sentence of stem) before reading options.

### Mini-Passage: Mangrove Restoration

Mangrove replanting programmes accelerated after the 2004 Indian Ocean tsunami demonstrated coastal buffer value. Early projects prioritized monoculture stands of single species for ease of counting survival rates, yet ecologists later documented that mixed-species plots better resisted pest outbreaks and stored more carbon per hectare. Funding agencies initially demanded short-term survival metrics; researchers argued that functional recovery—fish nursery return, sediment stabilisation—required decade-scale evaluation windows ill-suited to electoral budget cycles.

**Questions 8–14**

8. MCQ: Monoculture was initially favoured because  
   A. it stored maximum carbon  
   B. it simplified measurement of survival ✓  
   C. mixed species were illegal  
   D. tsunamis destroyed mixed plots  

9. MCQ: Researchers believed functional recovery assessment needed  
   A. weeks  
   B. decades ✓  
   C. no evaluation  
   D. electoral approval only  

10. Choose TWO: Benefits of mixed-species plots mentioned:  
    A. pest resistance ✓  
    B. higher carbon storage ✓  
    C. lower cost always  
    D. faster tsunamis  

11–14. Identify distractor type for: (11) option copying "tsunami" (12) option "always lower cost" (13) option reversing decades to weeks (14) option "mixed species illegal"

**Solutions:** 8.B 9.B 10.A+B 11.word-copy 12.not stated 13.opposite 14.not stated/extreme

## Additional Passage 2: Deep-Sea Mining Regulation

The International Seabed Authority issues exploration contracts for polymetallic nodules in the Clarion-Clipperton Zone. Contractors must submit environmental impact assessments, yet benthic ecosystems remain poorly catalogued, complicating baseline definitions. Small-island states dependent on tuna fisheries fear sediment plumes will migrate into pelagic zones. A moratorium proposal backed by several European parliaments awaits consensus that mining corporations resist, citing demand for cobalt in battery supply chains.

**MCQ:**

21. Exploration contracts concern  
    A. continental shelf oil  
    B. polymetallic nodules ✓  
    C. coral reef restoration  
    D. tidal energy  

22. Environmental baselines are difficult because  
    A. ecosystems are well mapped  
    B. benthic life is insufficiently catalogued ✓

    D. tuna prohibit measurement  

23. Choose TWO concerns mentioned:  
    A. Sediment plumes ✓  
    B. Cobalt demand context ✓  
    C. Complete mining ban enacted  
    D. Space mining  

**Solutions:** 21.B 22.B 23.A+B

## MCQ and Band Descriptors

Band 8–9 Reading requires "dealing with unfamiliar topics" — MCQ on technical passages tests whether you extract logic without mastering vocabulary. **Define unknown terms by context** rather than skipping question.
