## Cohesion is logical visibility
Cohesion helps the reader follow your reasoning. It is not a competition to use the largest number of linkers. A paragraph is cohesive when its sentences answer a sequence: claim, reason, example, consequence. Repeating a key noun selectively is often clearer than replacing it with an ambiguous synonym.

## Four cohesive tools
**Reference:** “This policy” must point to one identifiable policy.  
**Substitution:** use *this approach, such a measure,* or *the former* when the reference is unmistakable.  
**Conjunction:** choose *however, therefore, for example,* and *although* for their real logical function.  
**Lexical chains:** repeat a controlled family: *housing, rent, tenants, affordability*, rather than jumping among unrelated fancy synonyms.

## Lexical resource
Band 7 vocabulary is generally flexible and accurate. Band 8–9 vocabulary is more precise and natural, with rare slips. Precision means writing *mitigate traffic congestion* rather than *make traffic badness lower*. Collocation matters: *pose a threat, allocate funding, draw a distinction, address a shortfall, impose a restriction*.

## Repair exercise
Weak: “Firstly, people use phones. Moreover, phones are bad. On the other hand, phones are useful.”  
Better: “Constant phone use can fragment attention, particularly when notifications interrupt sustained study. Nevertheless, the same devices allow students to access course materials and contact tutors quickly. Their value therefore depends on whether users can control interruptions.”
