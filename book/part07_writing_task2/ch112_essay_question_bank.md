## How to use this bank
Plan rather than memorise. For each question, identify the type, write a one-sentence thesis, select two ideas, and test whether you answer every instruction. Rotate topics and question types. All questions below are original practice prompts.

## Deliberate-practice rotation
Choose ten questions per week: two opinion, two discussion, two problem–solution, two advantage–disadvantage, and two double questions. Write full essays for three, outlines for four, and introductions plus theses for three. Revisit weak topics after feedback.
